How to get your HoyoLab Cookies

These cookies authenticate your account safely with Hoyoverse. You only need to copy them once!

1

Log into HoyoLab in your Web Browser

Open hoyolab.com in Google Chrome, Edge, or Brave, and log into your account.

2

Open Developer Tools

Press F12 (or right click anywhere → choose Inspect).

3

Find Cookies in the Application Tab

Click on the Application tab at the top → expand Cookies on the left side → click on https://www.hoyolab.com.

4

Copy these 4 Values:

Cookie Name Description
ltuid_v2 Your HoyoLab Account ID number (e.g. 123456789)
ltoken_v2 Your authentication token (long string starting with v2_...)
cookie_token_v2 Verification token (long string)
account_id_v2 Usually matches your ltuid_v2
Configure Variables on Vercel

In your Vercel Dashboard, go to your project → Settings → Environment Variables, and add each of these:

Variable Key Value Action
LTUID_V2 Paste your ltuid_v2 value
LTOKEN_V2 Paste your ltoken_v2 value
COOKIE_TOKEN_V2 Paste your cookie_token_v2
ACCOUNT_ID_V2 Paste your account_id_v2
ENABLE_GENSHIN true
ENABLE_STARRAIL true
ENABLE_ZZZ true
Privacy guarantee: These values are encrypted directly by Vercel. Only your own serverless function can read them.
